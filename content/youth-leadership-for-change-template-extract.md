## Youth 4 Elders — Program description

**Label:** Youth Leaders  

**Paragraph:**  
Youth 4 Elders is a student-led movement at the University of Ottawa. We moved beyond simple volunteering to create a cycle of mutual growth. By blending structured seminars with creative wellness programs, we gave students the autonomy to design solutions that directly addressed the educational, social, and emotional needs of Ottawa's aging population.

## Youth leadership (right column)

**Heading:** Youth Leadership  

**Paragraph:**  
Youth 4 Elders is the core of the initiative, leading and managing all aspects of the organization through a student executive team. They design and run workshops, coordinate volunteers, organize fundraising events, and lead advocacy campaigns focused on senior care. With structured mentorship and hands-on experience, youth develop leadership, communication, and project management skills while taking real responsibility for creating meaningful impact in the lives of older adults.
